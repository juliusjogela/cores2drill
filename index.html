What we do

Drilling, vents and cutting.

Core drilling for heating, plumbing, electrical and ventilation, in sizes from 40mm to 350mm.

Diamond core drilling

Precise round holes through walls, floors and ceilings, with far less dust, noise and vibration than breaking out by hand.

  • Hole sizes from 40mm to 350mm
  • Concrete, reinforced concrete, block and brick
  • Heating, plumbing, drainage and electrical
  • Ducting, flues and extraction

Wall vent installation Best seller

Our most popular service. We core the opening and fit the vent, so the room gets the ventilation it needs with a neat finish inside and out.

  • Drilled and fitted in one visit
  • Vents that meet SEAI retrofit requirements
  • Single houses or whole schemes
More on wall vents

Stitch drilling & openings

When a single core isn't big enough, we drill a line of overlapping holes to form doorways, service openings and trenches through thick concrete.

  • Openings through walls and slabs
  • Floor trenches for services
  • Any shape, set out to your marks

Wall vents & retrofit

Wall vents for retrofit contractors.

Wall vent installation is our best seller. A large share of that work is for retrofit contractors, where homes being insulated and upgraded need proper background ventilation to pass.

  • SEAI-compliant vents. We supply and fit wall vents that meet SEAI requirements for retrofit works.
  • Cored, not hacked. A clean round hole through the wall, with the vent fitted and finished inside and out.
  • Built for volume. One house or a full scheme, we work through your list and keep to your programme.
  • Homeowners welcome. Need a single vent for a room, a stove or a dryer? We do those too.

About

A subcontractor that turns up and gets it done.
