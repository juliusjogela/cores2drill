Privacy & cookies

Last updated 16 November 2026.

Who we are

Cores2Drill is a diamond core drilling business based in Galway, Ireland. If you have any question about this page or your information, email info@cores2drill.ie.

When you contact us

This website has no forms and no accounts. If you call, WhatsApp or email us, we use your name, number, email and job details only to reply, quote and do the work, and to keep normal business and tax records. We don't sell or share them for marketing.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies unless you press Accept on the cookie banner. If you accept, Google Ads sets cookies so we can see which of our ads led someone to tap our phone, WhatsApp or email links. We don't use them for remarketing or to build a profile of you. If you press Reject, Google's tag isn't loaded at all.

Your choice is remembered on your device for 6 months. You can change it any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Other services the site uses

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download them.
  • The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp, which is run by Meta under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing info@cores2drill.ie. If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.
